# RTL8192FU

## Captured Wireless Card

- D-Link AN3U rev. A1, `2001:3328`: 2.4 GHz, Wi-Fi 4, 2T2R, USB 2.0 high speed.
- EFUSE on the captured card: chip cut 1, UMC, RFE type 5, crystal cap 0x25.
- Cold-boot captures: `driver_captures/captures_rtl8xxxu_8192fu/capture-{1,2,3}.pcap` (Kali 6.19.14). Each has probe, `airmon-ng start`, a 10 s airodump hop, `iw` channels 1–14, and two aireplay-ng injection-test + directed-deauth passes on channel 1.

## Linux Driver Source

- Upstream: `git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/stable/linux.git`, mainline `rtl8xxxu` (not DKMS).
- Pinned: v6.19.14, commit `b9dbb4576bc8c69e87b4ca4caa2fb1c0db47d722` (2026-04-22), the captured vermagic.
- Vendored: `driver_sources/rtl8xxxu-source-v6.19.14/` (`8192f.c`, `core.c`, `regs.h`, `rtl8xxxu.h`).
- `driver_sources/rtl8xxxu-source-v7.2.6/` is also vendored. Its only wire difference for this chip is the C2H hardware-feature dump in `rtl8xxxu_init_device` (write 0xfd to 0x1a0 before firmware download, poll 0x19, read 13 bytes, write 0), which only decides HT40 advertisement.

## Python Port Details

- `chips/rtl8192fu/__init__.py` claims all six `rtl8192fu_fops` VID:PIDs from core.c's device table.
- Status: `verify_pcap.py` reproduces every vendor op of all three captures (about 20,100 each) with zero waivers. That covers probe, start, configure_filter, 60 tunes and 502 TX URBs per capture, including endpoints, and every bulk-IN buffer decodes. On hardware: cold and warm bring-up, RX on channels 1–14, hopping.
- Module layout follows the source: `core.py` = core.c, `rtl8192f.py` = 8192f.c (+ `rtl8188f_channel_to_group` / `rtl8188f_phy_lc_calibrate` from 8188f.c), `iqk.py` = the 8192f IQK and the core.c helpers it calls, `rx.py` / `tx.py` = core.c RX/TX.
- `constants.py` and `tables.py` are generated by `scripts/chips/rtl8192fu/extract_source.py`; constants are the regs.h / rtl8xxxu.h `#define`s the package references.

### Non-obvious in the port

- `Rtl8xxxuFileops` mirrors `struct rtl8xxxu_fileops`; core.c functions call chip code only through `priv.fops`, as in the C.
- `needs_full_init = 1`: every bring-up is the full cold path. A chip left warm by a session that never called `close()` re-initialises through the same path; `rtl8xxxu_download_firmware` resets the running 8051.
- `rtl8xxxu_init_queue_priority` keeps the C's `vop = viq ^ 3`, so VO and VI share OUT endpoint 0x06.
- `MONITOR_FILTER_FLAGS` (`FIF_BCN_PRBRESP_PROMISC | FIF_CONTROL | FIF_OTHER_BSS | FIF_PSPOLL`) is what mac80211 passed to `configure_filter` in the captures (RCR 0x7000702f). `FIF_CONTROL` is why ACKs reach RX with no extra write.
- The LED is in hardware TX/RX-event mode from probe (`rtl8xxxu_init_led`), and set to LED_OFF on `close()`.
- The 2 s `rtl8xxxu_watchdog_callback` is ported and runs. While unassociated it writes only if the crystal cap has drifted from the EFUSE default or ATC is off, so it is silent in all three captures.
- C2H packets (`rpt_sel`) are queued and handled by `rtl8xxxu_c2hcmd_callback` under the I/O lock. A BT_INFO C2H issues an H2C plus coex-table writes; none occur in the captures.
- RX signal is the C's s8 arithmetic, e.g. CCK pwdb 127 reports +17 dBm.

## Known Problems

- `rtl8xxxu_stop` and `power_off` are not pcap-verified: the captures end before `airmon-ng stop`'s register writes.
- Reference-AP beacon rate on ch1 measured 6.1–6.8/s (3×60 s), against about 9.8/s for a known-good card on another day. The PR #121 driver measured 6.0–7.4/s in alternating runs at the same moment, and no second card was attached for a same-moment baseline.
- Associated-station paths (`rtl8xxxu_track_cfo` CFO tracking, rate-adaptation iteration) are ported only up to the unassociated branch; wifit3 never associates.

## Driver Entry Points

- Probe: `core.rtl8xxxu_probe` → `rtl8xxxu_init_device`.
- Monitor bring-up: `core.start_monitor_interface` (`rtl8xxxu_start`, `rtl8xxxu_configure_filter`, `rtl8xxxu_config`).
- Channel tune: `core.rtl8xxxu_config` → `rtl8192f.rtl8192f_set_tx_power` + `rtl8192fu_config_channel`.
- TX: `tx.rtl8xxxu_tx` → `(endpoint, URB)`.
- RX: `rx.rtl8xxxu_parse_rxdesc24`, `rx.jaguar2_rx_parse_phystats`.
- Teardown: `driver.RTL8192FUDriver._disconnect` (`rtl8xxxu_deinit_led`, `rtl8xxxu_stop`, `rtl8192fu_power_off`).
- USB: `transport.RTL8192FUTransport` (`rtl8xxxu_parse_usb`, register I/O, bulk-OUT).

## Scripts

- `scripts/chips/rtl8192fu/verify_pcap.py [capture-N]`: the single-cursor replay gate (wire ops, TX, RX).
- `scripts/chips/rtl8192fu/extract_source.py`: regenerates `tables.py` and `constants.py` from the pinned source.
- `scripts/chips/rtl8192fu/test_hw.py`: RX-only cold-boot smoke test on one channel.
- `scripts/chips/rtl8192fu/test_tx_timing.py`: live directed-deauth timing and ACK tally (transmits; user-run only).
